Engineering & privacy standards

Three commitments we hold every release to.

Local-first

As a guest, your data stays on your device. Reminders work offline on Android.

Encrypted sync

Sync is optional. Secure notes are encrypted on your device with AES-GCM, passwords are hashed on-device, and you can delete your data anytime.

No ads, no analytics or telemetry, and no paywalls. Every feature is free, permanently.
