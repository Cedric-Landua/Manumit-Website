3. Local-First Architecture & Cloud Sync

Split Task operates on a local-first data model. You acknowledge and understand the operational mechanics of your data:

3.1 Local Storage & Offline Usage

  • Device Storage: The Application stores your tasks, calendar events, categories, and settings locally on your device via localStorage and browser storage.
  • Guest / Device-Only Mode: If you use the App in device-only mode (without creating an account), your data remains exclusively on your physical device. Uninstalling the App or clearing browser data will permanently erase your data. We have no ability to recover offline-only data.

3.2 Cloud Syncing & Authentication

  • Full-Set Replacement Sync: When signed into an account, your data syncs across devices using a full-set replacement model. The backend server maintains your state by replacing server-side datasets with the complete list of items, categories, and settings pushed by your active device during sync operations.
  • Client-Side Hashing: Passwords are hashed client-side using SHA-256 via the Web Crypto API prior to transmission. We do not store or process plaintext passwords.
  • Automated Data Maintenance: If enabled in your App settings, automated background routines (including server cron jobs) may push incomplete task deadlines forward or permanently purge deleted items based on your configured delete_after_days retention rule.

4. User Accounts & Termination

  • Account Deletion: You may permanently delete your account and associated cloud data at any time via the in-app Delete Account flow under Settings. Account deletion is executed as an immediate batch transaction on the server, permanently purging your user record, items, categories, and settings.
  • Termination by Developer: We reserve the right to suspend or terminate your account or restrict access to backend sync services at our sole discretion, without notice, if we reasonably believe you have violated this EULA or engaged in abusive network behavior.
